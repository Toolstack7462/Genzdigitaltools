# Product

## Register

brand

> Default register is **brand** — the public marketing site (`genzdigitalstore.com`):
> homepage, services, portfolio, pricing, about, contact, and the public-facing
> signup/login. The project also has a **product** surface — the member dashboard
> and admin panel on `app.genzdigitalstore.com` — which serves workflows, not
> marketing. When working on those surfaces, override the register to `product`.

## Users

- **Prospects** browsing the public site: creators, students, freelancers, and small
  businesses evaluating whether to buy a membership. Context: deciding fast, often on
  mobile, comparing against doing it themselves or hiring a generic agency. Job to be
  done: understand what they get, trust it's legitimate and premium, and start (sign up
  or reach out on WhatsApp).
- **Members** using the app: people who have paid and log in to open their assigned
  tools and manage services. Job: get to a working tool in one click.
- **Admins/support**: manage tools, members, assignments, and the extension release.

## Product Purpose

Gen Z Digital Store is a premium digital platform that bundles **members-only access to
90+ professional tools** (AI, SEO, design, productivity) with **done-for-you creative and
technical services** (web & app development, branding, social media, SEO, writing,
business documents) under one trusted brand. A Chrome extension opens assigned tools with
authorized sessions in one click. Success = a prospect understands the offer in seconds,
trusts the brand enough to sign up or message on WhatsApp, and members reach a working
tool without friction.

## Brand Personality

Premium, trusted, modern. Confident and polished like a high-end SaaS/agency, never
hype-y or bargain-bin. Voice is clear and human (no jargon, no AI filler, no em-dash
overuse), warm enough to feel approachable but precise enough to feel expert. The brand's
energy comes from craft — typography, spacing, motion, and a disciplined navy/teal/blue
identity — not from loud color or gimmicks.

## Anti-references

Explicitly NOT a **generic AI/SaaS template**: no cream-and-gradient backgrounds, no
endless identical icon-card grids, no tiny uppercase tracked eyebrow above every section,
no numbered `01 / 02 / 03` section scaffolding by reflex, no gradient text, no
glassmorphism-as-default. Also not a cheap/cluttered tool-reseller marketplace. If a
prospect could glance at a section and think "AI made that," it has failed.

## Design Principles

- **Trust is the conversion.** Every section should make the platform feel legitimate,
  secure, and premium — that's what turns a browser into a member. Show proof and clarity
  before persuasion.
- **One clear next step per screen.** Guide toward a single primary action (Get Started →
  signup, Member Login, or WhatsApp); secondary actions stay visually subordinate.
- **Human, not generated.** Copy reads like a confident person wrote it. Avoid AI tells in
  both language and layout (see anti-references). Specific claims over filler.
- **Craft carries the brand.** Differentiation comes from disciplined type, spacing,
  motion, and the navy/teal/blue system — not from novelty effects.
- **Consistent across surfaces.** Public site, auth pages, dashboard, and admin share one
  visual language so the experience feels like one premium product end to end.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.1 AA**: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast (large text ≥3:1), visible keyboard
focus states, full keyboard navigation, semantic headings, labelled controls, and a
`prefers-reduced-motion` alternative for every animation. Color is never the only signal
(pair with icon/text). Touch targets ≥44px on mobile.
